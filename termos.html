1. What this app is

CortEgames Agendador is a private scheduling tool operated by the owner of the CortEgames channels (TikTok @cortegames25, the CortEgames Facebook Page and the CortEgames YouTube channel). It publishes short gaming videos edited by the owner to the owner's own accounts at scheduled times. It is not offered to the public and no third party can sign up or connect an account.

The app uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and Content Posting API) only to read the basic profile of the connected account and to publish videos to that same account, with the owner's authorization. 3. Content

The account owner is responsible for every video and caption published through the app and for having the rights needed to publish them. The app does not create, buy or sell content on behalf of others.

4. Availability

The app is provided as is, without guarantees of availability. Scheduled posts may be delayed or fail if TikTok, Google or the internet connection are unavailable.

5. Ending use

The owner can disconnect the TikTok account at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions). After disconnection the app stops publishing and deletes its access token.

6. Contact

Questions about these terms: send a direct message to @cortegames25 on TikTok or to the CortEgames Page on Facebook.
